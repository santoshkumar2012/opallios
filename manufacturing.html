Helping Manufacturers Focus on What Matters Most

Our team of experts brings clarity to manufacturing - connecting people, data, and processes to keep production running smoothly and profitably. Our Workforce and Spend Management solutions simplify how you manage people, costs, and processes — improving productivity, reducing waste, and keeping your operations running at their best.

Challenges in Manufacturing Workforce Management

  • Shift Planning and Labor Costs: Unplanned overtime, rising labor expenses, and inefficient shift schedules can drain productivity and impact margins.
  • Skills and Training: As technology and automation evolve, teams need ongoing training to stay adaptable and ready for what’s next.
  • Compliance Requirements: Meeting OSHA standards, labor laws, and union agreements takes accurate tracking and reliable reporting — every time.
  • Disconnected Data: When systems don’t talk to each other, it’s hard to get a clear view of workforce performance and make confident decisions.

Challenges in Manufacturing Spend Management

  • Procurement Inefficiencies: Manual sourcing and outdated vendor processes slow things down and drive costs up.
  • Uncontrolled Spending: Unapproved or off-contract purchases throw budgets off and complicate inventory management.
  • Vendor Risk and Compliance: Keeping suppliers aligned with quality, safety, and performance standards takes constant attention and oversight.
  • Limited Visibility: When spend data is scattered, it’s hard to spot savings opportunities or negotiate better supplier terms.
