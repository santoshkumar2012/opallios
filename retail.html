Retail at a Crossroads

Modern retailers are navigating razor-thin margins, evolving customer expectations, labor volatility, and global supply chain disruptions. Managing workforce productivity and optimizing spend are no longer back-office functions—they are central to competitive advantage.

Workforce Management Challenges in Retail

  • Seasonal Staffing Volatility: High employee turnover and seasonal demand spikes make staffing unpredictable and costly.
  • Scheduling Inefficiencies: Manual or disconnected systems cause overstaffing or understaffing during peak hours.
  • Lack of Real-Time Labor Insights: Inability to track productivity by store, department, or employee in real time.
  • Compliance Risks: Regulations around overtime, breaks, and labor laws vary by location and require accurate tracking.

Spend Management Challenges in Retail

  • Fragmented Supplier Relationships: Sourcing from multiple vendors across categories leads to reduced negotiation leverage.
  • Lack of Procurement Visibility: Store-level purchases and indirect spend are often unmanaged and untracked.
  • Inventory Overspend or Shortages: Poor coordination between demand forecasting and procurement results in either bloated inventory or stockouts.
  • Inefficient Marketing & Promotional Spend: Disconnected spending leads to unclear ROI on campaigns and loyalty programs.
