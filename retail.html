The Real Challenge of Modern Retail: Higher Complexity, Smarter Solutions

Margins are tight, shoppers are restless, and the rules keep changing. From staffing to spending, what used to happen in the back office now defines who wins on the floor.

Workforce Management Challenges in Retail

  • Seasonal Staffing Swings: Retail moves fast — and so does your workforce. High turnover and seasonal surges make it tough to plan ahead without overspending.
  • Scheduling Gaps: Disconnected tools and manual schedules often lead to overstaffing on slow days and not enough coverage when it counts most.
  • Limited Labor Visibility: Without real-time insights by store, department, or employee, it’s hard to know where productivity is gained — or lost.
  • Compliance Complexity: From overtime and breaks to regional labor laws, compliance can’t be left to guesswork. One error can mean costly setbacks.

Spend Management Challenges in Retail

  • Too Many Vendors, Too Little Leverage: When you’re juggling suppliers across categories, it’s hard to keep costs in check — or negotiate from a position of strength.
  • No Clear View of What’s Being Spent: Store teams make quick buys, indirect expenses add up, and suddenly your spend picture looks more like a puzzle than a plan.
  • Inventory That’s Never Quite Right: Too much stock eats up cash. Too little means empty shelves and lost sales. It’s a delicate balance — and hard to nail without the right insight.
  • Marketing Money That’s Hard to Track: Campaigns, promos, loyalty spend — it’s all moving fast, but it’s not always clear what’s actually driving results.
